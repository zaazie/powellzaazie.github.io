About

I believe the most meaningful tech work happens closest to the communities that need it. So I stayed in Lawra — and I'm building from here.

My career spans data analytics at the Electoral Commission of Ghana, community management at Yellow Card Financial (educating 1,000+ users across 10+ African countries), and IT & Programmes at the National Youth Authority. I hold a BSc in Computer Science from UENR and I'm completing an MSc in Innovation & Entrepreneurship at SDD-UBIDS.

I founded ZenceNews as UENR-Vibes — now a social news platform for Ghana's student communities. The Zaazie Initiative curates fellowships, grants, and opportunities specifically for youth in Ghana. Antonata connects traditional weavers to global markets. Each project is a bet on the idea that talent in the Upper West Region deserves the same opportunities as anywhere else.
